Privacy Policy

GameNomad is a game deals website. This policy explains what happens with your information when you visit it. Last updated November 09, 2026.

What we collect

The site has no accounts, comment forms or newsletters, and we do not ask for personal data. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as your IP address and browser type under GitHub's own privacy statement.

Third-party links

Game links lead to third-party stores (Instant Gaming, IndieGala). When you click one, that store may set cookies and track the visit so a commission can be credited to us. Their privacy policies apply once you leave this site.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

Because we do not store personal data about visitors, there is normally nothing to access or delete. For any privacy question contact zeddev@duck.com.
