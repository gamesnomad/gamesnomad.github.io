Privacy Policy

Official Standard Documentation · Last updated: November 09, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GameNomad (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, processes, and protects your information when you visit and browse our website. We are firmly committed to user privacy, transparency, and data minimization in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK Data Protection Act, and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA).

1. Zero Direct Personal Data Collection

GameNomad is a public, registration-free promotional catalog.

  • We do not operate user accounts, membership logins, or profiles.
  • We do not collect names, phone numbers, postal addresses, or payment card details.
  • We do not maintain public comment sections or web forums that prompt for personal details.
  • We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information to data brokers.

2. Server Logs and Technical Telemetry

Our website is statically generated and hosted via GitHub Pages (provided by GitHub, Inc. and Microsoft Corporation). When you access any page on GameNomad, the hosting infrastructure automatically records standard HTTP access logs containing:

  • Your Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • Browser user-agent string, operating system, and architecture
  • Referring website URL and target page requested
  • Date and precise timestamp of the HTTP request

These server logs are processed solely to ensure infrastructure security, detect malicious automated traffic, mitigate Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks, and maintain network reliability in accordance with GitHub's Privacy Statement.

3. Outbound Links and Third-Party Affiliate Merchants

When you click on deal links, pricing buttons, or promotional cards on GameNomad, you are redirected to third-party digital game storefronts (including Instant Gaming and IndieGala). These external merchants operate under their own independent terms and privacy notices:

  • When navigating to an affiliate store, a tracking identifier (such as igr=gamenomadbot or ref=gamenomadbot) is passed via the URL query parameters to credit promotional referrals.
  • The external retailer may store cookies, local storage tokens, or session identifiers on your device to finalize cart transactions and attribute verified referral commissions.
  • We have no control over the privacy practices, tracking mechanisms, or data collection performed on third-party domains. We strongly recommend reviewing the privacy notice of any merchant before concluding a purchase.

4. Children's Online Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Article 8)

Our site is directed to general gaming audiences aged 13 and older (or 16 and older in jurisdictions governed by European data protection rules). We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or process information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a minor has submitted personal information through our contact channels, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Under applicable international privacy frameworks (including GDPR Articles 15–22 and CCPA California Civil Code § 1798.100 et seq.), you retain legal rights concerning your data:

  • Right of Access & Portability: To request confirmation of any data processed.
  • Right to Erasure (“Right to be Forgotten”): To request immediate deletion of personal correspondence.
  • Right to Object: To object to data processing activities.

Because GameNomad does not retain visitor profiles or personal accounts, email communications directed to our support inbox represent the only personal data retained. To exercise any data subject rights, contact us at zeddev@duck.com.
