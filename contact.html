2. DMCA Copyright & Takedown Procedure

GameNomad respects the intellectual property rights of game developers, publishers, and artists. All cover images and promotional media are aggregated from authorized partner feeds and public retail pages for non-commercial identification purposes.

If you are a copyright owner or an authorized agent and believe that content hosted on GameNomad infringes upon your copyright, please deliver a formal written notification pursuant to the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3)) containing:

  1. A physical or electronic signature of a person authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed, or a representative list of such works.
  3. Identification of the specific URL on GameNomad containing the material to be removed.
  4. Sufficient contact information, including your full legal name, telephone number, and physical or email address.
  5. A statement that you have a good faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. A statement that the information in the notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner.

Please send all takedown notices to zeddev@duck.com. We will investigate and expeditiously remove or disable access to infringing material.
