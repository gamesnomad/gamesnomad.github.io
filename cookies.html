Cookie Policy

Official Standard Documentation · Last updated: November 09, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how cookies, tracking tokens, and local browser storage technologies operate on GameNomad.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device by websites that you visit. They are widely used to make websites function efficiently, remember user preferences, and provide analytical telemetry to site operators.

2. Cookies on GameNomad

GameNomad does not set first-party tracking, profiling, or analytical advertising cookies.

We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or third-party behavioral advertising scripts that monitor your browsing activities across unrelated websites.

We may utilize your browser's local storage (localStorage) strictly for technical, user-initiated preferences, such as:

  • Remembering your dismissed deal spotlight popup so you are not repeatedly prompted during the same browsing session.
  • Saving client-side filter preferences (such as selected platform or sorting view).

3. Third-Party Merchant Referral Cookies

When you click on an external deal link to Instant Gaming or IndieGala, the retailer's destination site will place cookies on your device according to their respective cookie policies. These affiliate attribution cookies allow the store to identify GameNomad as the referring portal if you proceed with an order within the merchant's tracking window (typically 24 hours to 30 days).

4. Managing and Disabling Cookies

You have the absolute right to accept, reject, or delete cookies at any time via your browser settings. You can find detailed instructions for popular browsers below:

  • Google Chrome
  • Apple Safari
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Microsoft Edge
